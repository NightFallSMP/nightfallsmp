📜 Server Rules

1.No cheats of any kind (texture packs, mods, clients, etc.).▸
This includes hacked clients, X-ray texture packs, or any modifications that give unfair advantages in combat or resource gathering.
2.No overpowered or illegal items (overpowered = Elytra + mace).▸
Combining Elytra flight with the mace creates an unfair combat advantage. Other illegal item combinations will be announced by staff.
3.No exploiting glitches.▸
Report any bugs or glitches to staff immediately. Using them for personal advantage will result in punishment.
4.Follow staff orders.▸
When staff asks you to do or stop doing something, comply immediately. Disrespecting or ignoring staff instructions is not tolerated.
5.No lag machines.▸
Building contraptions designed to lag the server ruins the experience for everyone and will result in an immediate ban.
6.No cross-trading or real-world trading.▸
Don't trade in-game items or currency for real money, items from other servers, or external services. Keep all trades within the server economy.
7.No alt abuse.▸
Using alternate accounts to gain unfair advantages, bypass bans, or store extra items is prohibited. One player, one account.
8.No X-ray or ore-finding of any kind (mods, packs, clients, etc.).▸
Any method to see ores through blocks or locate them unfairly is strictly forbidden. Mine legitimately or face consequences.
9.No using Elytra before or during combat.▸
Elytra gives an unfair mobility advantage in PvP. Remove it before engaging in or being engaged in combat.
10.No debuffs in combat (unless used to counter Mace).▸
Weakness, slowness, and other debuff potions are banned in PvP except when defending against mace attacks to keep combat fair.
11.No ruining spawn of any kind.▸
Spawn is a shared space for all players. Don't grief, lava cast, or destroy the spawn area in any way.
12.No threatening to ruin the spawn/server.▸
Even joking about destroying spawn or crashing the server will be taken seriously and may result in punishment.
13.No using bots, AI, or macros to farm.▸
AFK farming with automated systems is unfair. All farming must be done actively by a real player at their keyboard.
14.No using the server for personal gain.▸
Don't promote your social media, advertise services, or use the server as a platform for personal profit outside of normal gameplay.
15.No stashing illegal items (hiding them from staff/members).▸
If you have banned items, don't hide them. Turn them in to staff immediately. Concealing illegal items makes you complicit.
16.No pushing players outside of arena.▸
Fights should happen where they're supposed to. Don't use knockback or pistons to force players out of designated PvP areas.
17.No encouraging players to break a rule.▸
Telling others to cheat, exploit, or break rules makes you just as guilty. Don't be a bad influence.
18.Report players when caught breaking rules.▸
If you see someone cheating or breaking rules, report them to staff with evidence. Help keep the server fair for everyone.
19.No bed camping (camping a player's bed to farm them for hearts/kills).▸
Waiting at someone's spawn point to repeatedly kill them is toxic behavior and exploitative. Give players a fair chance.
20.No staff impersonation.▸
Don't pretend to be staff, use similar names, or claim to have authority you don't have. This confuses players and undermines trust.
21.No ban evasion.▸
If you're banned, don't use alts or VPNs to get back on. Accept your punishment and appeal properly if you believe it was unfair.
22.No naked killing.▸
Don't kill players who clearly have no gear or items. It's pointless griefing that ruins the new player experience.
23.Use common sense.▸
If something feels wrong, manipulative, or unfair, it probably is. Think before you act and consider how your actions affect others.

💬 Chat Rules

1.No spamming or flooding.▸
Don't send repetitive messages, excessive caps, or flood chat with nonsense. Give everyone a chance to communicate.
2.No mass pinging (members or staff).▸
Don't ping everyone or multiple staff members unnecessarily. It's annoying and disruptive. Ping only when truly needed.
3.No advertising or promotion.▸
Don't promote other servers, Discord servers, websites, or social media without permission. This includes DM advertising.
4.No chat colors.▸
Colored text in chat can be distracting and is reserved for staff announcements. Keep your messages in default white.
5.No NSFW content/context of any kind.▸
Keep all content family-friendly. No sexual, graphic, or inappropriate content in any form. This is a community for all ages.
6.No harassing others.▸
Treat everyone with respect. Bullying, targeting, or repeatedly bothering someone will not be tolerated.
7.No racism, discrimination, or hate speech.▸
Zero tolerance for slurs, bigotry, or hateful comments about race, religion, gender, sexuality, or any other identity.
8.No threats or encouraging real-life harm.▸
Never threaten violence, self-harm, or encourage others to hurt themselves or others. This results in immediate action.
9.No sharing personal information.▸
Don't share your own or others' addresses, phone numbers, full names, or other sensitive info. Protect your privacy and others'.
10.No blackmail or impersonation of staff.▸
Don't threaten to expose information or pretend to be staff members. Both are serious offenses that break trust.
11.No spreading rumors or false accusations that could hurt a staff member's reputation.▸
Making up lies about staff or spreading unverified claims damages reputations. If you have concerns, report them properly.
12.No false reports.▸
Don't file fake reports to get others in trouble. False reporting wastes staff time and can get you punished instead.
13.No false tickets (opening tickets without solid reasons).▸
Only create support tickets when you have a legitimate issue or question. Don't waste staff time with joke tickets.
14.No exploiting rule loopholes.▸
Don't try to find ways around the rules by technicalities. Follow the spirit of the rules, not just the letter.
15.Follow staff instructions.▸
When staff tells you to stop or do something, listen and comply. Arguing or ignoring them will only make things worse.
16.Use common sense.▸
If you're wondering whether something is okay to say, it probably isn't. Think before you type and be respectful.
