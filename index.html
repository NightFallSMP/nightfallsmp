How to Join

Follow these simple steps to start your NightFallSMP adventure!

1

Launch Minecraft

Open your Minecraft launcher and make sure you're running Java Edition. NightFallSMP requires Minecraft Java Edition version 1.20 or later. We recommend using the latest stable release for the best experience.

2

Add Server

Go to "Multiplayer" and click "Add Server". Enter "NightFallSMP" as the server name and paste NightFallSMP.valk.rocks as the server address. Click "Done" to save the server.

3

Join Discord

Before you start playing, we highly recommend joining our Discord server at discord.gg/yUzRtwt2sV. This is where you can get help from staff, trade with players, stay updated on server events, and become part of our community!

4

Read the Rules

Take a few minutes to review our Minecraft and Chat rules (available in the sidebar). Understanding these rules ensures you have a great experience and helps keep our community safe and fun for everyone. Breaking rules can result in warnings, kicks, or bans.

5

Start Playing!

Double-click the NightFallSMP server in your server list to join. You'll spawn at our protected spawn area where you can read more information, visit shops, and prepare for your adventure. Good luck, and may your hearts be plentiful!

Lifesteal System

The core mechanic of our server - every kill steals hearts from your opponent!

How it works:
  • When you kill another player, you steal one of their hearts permanently
  • When you die, you lose one heart to your killer
  • If you run out of hearts, you're temporarily banned until you can get more

  • Strategic combat is rewarded - choose your battles wisely!

📜 Minecraft Rules

Click on any rule to see a detailed explanation. Breaking these rules will result in punishments.

1. No cheats of any kind (texture packs, mods, clients, etc.)
▼
This means absolutely NO use of hacked clients, X-ray texture packs, modified game files, or any third-party software that gives you an unfair advantage. This includes but is not limited to: killaura, fly hacks, speed hacks, auto-clickers set above human speeds, ESP, tracers, or any mod that shows you information you shouldn't normally have access to. Allowed mods include Optifine, shaders, minimaps (without player/entity radar), and performance mods. When in doubt, ask staff before using any mod.
2. No overpowered or illegal items (no Elytra+Mace during fights, no Crystals or Anchors)
▼
End Crystals and Respawn Anchors are completely banned from PvP as they cause massive, unavoidable damage. The Mace weapon combined with Elytra creates an overpowered one-shot strategy that is not allowed during combat. Elytra itself cannot be worn or used during any PvP engagement. Any items obtained through duplication glitches or other exploits are considered illegal and must be reported to staff immediately.
3. No exploiting glitches
▼
If you discover a bug or glitch in Minecraft or our server plugins, you must report it to staff immediately - do not use it for personal gain. This includes duplication glitches, X-ray glitches, item duping, infinite money exploits, or any other unintended game behavior. Using glitches to gain advantages results in severe punishment including permanent bans. Players who report major glitches may receive rewards!
4. Follow staff orders
▼
When a staff member asks you to do (or stop doing) something, you must comply. This includes requests to come to a specific location for questioning, to stop certain behaviors, or to provide information. If you believe a staff member's order is unfair or incorrect, comply first and then report the issue to a higher-ranking staff member or server owner through Discord. Arguing with or ignoring staff will result in additional punishment.
5. No lag machines
▼
Creating devices or contraptions specifically designed to cause server lag is strictly prohibited. This includes excessive redstone clocks, massive entity farms, piston loops, or any builds that intentionally strain server performance. Even unintentional lag machines must be removed when discovered. Keep your farms reasonable in size and use proper chunk loading practices. Staff may ask you to reduce or remove builds that impact server performance.
6. No cross-trading or real-world trading
▼
You cannot trade in-game items, currency, or services for real money, items from other games/servers, or any other form of real-world value. This includes selling Minecraft accounts with server items, trading hearts for Discord Nitro, or any arrangement where real money or external value is exchanged for server resources. Buying/selling services (like building or grinding) for in-game currency only is allowed. Cross-server trading is also forbidden.
7. No alt abuse
▼
Using alternate Minecraft accounts to gain unfair advantages is forbidden. This includes: using alts to farm kills/hearts, circumvent death bans, hold extra inventory space, claim additional land, generate extra resources, or avoid punishments. You may have one alt account for legitimate purposes (like checking if you're online when you're AFK), but it cannot be used to give yourself any gameplay advantage. All accounts owned by you must be declared to staff.
8. No X-ray or ore-finding of any kind (mods, packs, clients, etc.)
▼
This rule specifically addresses tools that help you locate ores, spawners, or valuable blocks underground. X-ray texture packs, X-ray mods, cave finder tools, and any client modifications that reveal hidden blocks are completely banned. This also includes using external tools or seed readers to locate structures or resources. Mining must be done legitimately - staff have tools to detect suspicious mining patterns and will investigate unusual ore discovery rates.
9. No using Elytra before or during combat
▼
Once you engage in combat or are attacked by another player, you cannot equip or use Elytra for the entire duration of that combat encounter (including the combat timer). You also cannot wear Elytra as you initiate combat with someone. This prevents unfair escapes and keeps fights balanced. If you're wearing Elytra when unexpectedly attacked, you must remove it before fighting back. Using Elytra to flee from combat is considered combat logging.
10. No debuffs in combat (unless used to counter Mace)
▼
Using potions or effects that apply negative debuffs to your opponent (weakness, slowness, poison, etc.) is not allowed during PvP combat. The only exception is if your opponent is using a Mace weapon, in which case debuffs are permitted as a defensive counter-measure. This rule keeps combat skill-based rather than potion-spam based. Harming potions and damage-dealing effects are still allowed.
11. No ruining spawn of any kind
▼
The spawn area is protected and meant to be a safe, welcoming place for all players. Do not place blocks, break blocks, litter, create ugly structures, spam items, or do anything that damages the appearance or functionality of spawn. This includes pranks, "art," or any modifications without explicit staff permission. Violations result in immediate bans as spawn represents our server to new players.
12. No threatening to ruin the spawn/server
▼
Making threats to grief spawn, crash the server, or perform any destructive action against the server infrastructure is taken extremely seriously. This includes "joking" threats, vague threats, or threats made out of frustration. Even if you have no actual ability or intention to carry out the threat, making it is grounds for an immediate permanent ban. We have zero tolerance for threats against the server.
13. No using bots, AI, or macros to farm
▼
Automated farming using bots, scripts, macro programs, or AI is forbidden. All resource gathering, combat, and gameplay must be performed manually by a human player. This includes autoclickers for farming, auto-fish macros, auto-mining bots, or any automated tool that plays the game for you while you're AFK. Simple keybind rebinding is okay, but any automation that performs repeated actions is not allowed.
14. No using the server for personal gain
▼
The server is for playing Minecraft, not for advertising your social media, promoting your content, recruiting for other servers, or any other form of personal promotion. You cannot use the server's player base or Discord community to promote your YouTube channel, Twitch stream, or other projects without explicit staff permission. Asking people to follow/subscribe/donate in chat is not allowed.
15. No stashing illegal items (hiding them from staff/members)
▼
If you come into possession of illegal items (duped items, glitched items, items that shouldn't exist, etc.), you must report them to staff immediately - you cannot hide them or keep them "for later." Burying chests of illegal items, storing them in secret locations, or attempting to hide them in any way is considered just as bad as creating them yourself. This rule exists because hiding illegal items disrupts the economy and creates unfair advantages.
16. No pushing players outside of arena
▼
During organized PvP matches in designated arenas, you cannot use knockback, fishing rods, or any other method to push opponents outside the arena boundaries. Fights must stay within the designated combat zone. This rule ensures fair arena matches where the better fighter wins, not whoever can push someone into a pit first. Breaking this rule results in the match forfeit and potential ban from future events.
17. No encouraging players to break a rule
▼
Telling other players to break rules, teaching them how to break rules, or encouraging rule-breaking behavior makes you equally guilty. This includes "joking" about breaking rules, linking to hacked clients, explaining how to exploit glitches, or helping someone evade punishment. If you know someone is breaking rules, report them to staff - don't join them or help them. Facilitating rule-breaking results in the same punishment as breaking the rule yourself.
18. Report players when caught breaking rules
▼
If you witness someone breaking server rules, you should report them to staff through Discord tickets or by messaging an online staff member. Include evidence like screenshots, video, or detailed descriptions of what happened. False reports are punished, but genuine reports help keep the community safe. You can report anonymously if you prefer. Players who consistently report rule-breakers and help staff may receive recognition or rewards.
19. No bed camping (camping a player's bed to farm them for hearts/kills)
▼
Sitting at someone's respawn bed and repeatedly killing them as they spawn is considered harassment and unfair farming. While killing someone at their bed once or twice during a raid is acceptable, repeatedly spawn-killing them to farm hearts is forbidden. This creates a frustrating experience where the victim has no chance to defend themselves. If you kill someone at their bed, move on - don't camp it. Repeated bed camping results in heart penalties and bans.
20. No staff impersonation
▼
Pretending to be a staff member, using a similar name to staff, claiming to have staff powers, or in any way making other players believe you are part of the staff team is strictly forbidden. This includes "joking" claims of being staff, using color codes in your name to look like staff, or claiming you have mod/admin privileges. Impersonation confuses players and can be used to scam or manipulate others. This results in immediate permanent bans.
21. No ban evasion
▼
If you are banned from the server, you cannot use alternate accounts, VPNs, or any other method to bypass the ban and continue playing. This includes both temporary and permanent bans. Ban evasion results in all your accounts being permanently banned and IP banned. If you believe your ban was unfair, appeal it through proper channels on our Discord - don't attempt to evade it. Death bans (from losing all hearts) must be waited out or appealed legitimately.
22. No naked killing
▼
Attacking or killing players who have no armor or weapons equipped is considered unfair and unsportsmanlike. If you see someone clearly unprepared for combat (naked or in very basic gear), give them a chance to gear up or make it clear you want to fight. Repeatedly targeting defenseless players for easy kills/hearts is harassment. Exceptions include if the naked player attacks you first or enters a designated PvP zone. This rule promotes fair, skill-based combat.
23. Use common sense
▼
Not every possible bad behavior can be explicitly listed in the rules. Use your best judgment - if something seems like it would be unfair, harmful to the community, or exploitative, don't do it. This includes finding loopholes in rules, doing things that are "technically" allowed but clearly against the spirit of fair play, or arguing that something is okay because it's not specifically listed. Staff have final say on what constitutes rule-breaking behavior. When in doubt, ask staff first!

💬 Chat Rules

Click on any rule to see a detailed explanation. These rules apply to both in-game chat and Discord.

1. No spamming or flooding
▼
Sending the same message repeatedly, sending many messages in rapid succession, or posting nonsensical spam clutters chat and makes it hard for others to communicate. This includes repeating characters (AAAAAAA), sending multiple messages that could be combined into one, or copy-pasting the same text over and over. Occasional double messages are okay, but continuous flooding is not. Spam bots result in immediate permanent bans.
2. No mass pinging (members or staff)
▼
On Discord, do not tag @everyone, @here, or ping multiple staff members/players without a legitimate reason. Mass pinging disrupts everyone's notifications and is annoying. If you need staff help, ping the @Staff role once or use the ticket system - don't ping individual staff members repeatedly. Pinging many regular members for no reason is also not allowed. Only ping people when necessary and relevant to them.
3. No advertising or promotion
▼
Do not advertise other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, social media pages, YouTube channels, or any external content/services in our chat or Discord. This includes subtle promotion like "just started a new server, dm me for ip" or posting links to your content. Asking others to visit, join, or check out your stuff is advertising. The only exception is if staff explicitly gives you permission for a specific collaboration or event.
4. No chat colors
▼
Using color codes, formatting codes, or special characters to make your messages colored or formatted is reserved for staff announcements and important messages. Regular players should not use &-codes, Unicode characters for effects, or any method to make their messages stand out with colors or formatting. This keeps chat clean and ensures staff messages are easily identifiable. Ranks may have default colors, which are allowed.
5. No NSFW content/context of any kind
▼
This is a family-friendly server. Absolutely no sexual content, pornographic material, extremely violent/gory content, or anything inappropriate for younger audiences. This includes NSFW images, links, references, jokes, role-play, or discussions. Even "mild" inappropriate content is not allowed. ASCII art of inappropriate nature is banned. Skins with NSFW elements must be changed. This rule is strictly enforced with no warnings - violations result in immediate permanent bans.
6. No harassing others
▼
Harassment includes repeatedly bothering someone after they've asked you to stop, targeting specific players with insults or mockery, following them around to annoy them, or any behavior intended to make someone uncomfortable or upset. This includes in-game harassment (like repeatedly killing someone unfairly) and chat harassment. Friendly banter is okay, but if someone says "stop" or asks you to leave them alone, you must respect that. Continued harassment results in mutes and bans.
7. No racism, discrimination, or hate speech
▼
Any form of discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other personal characteristic is absolutely forbidden. This includes slurs, stereotypes, "jokes," hate symbols, or any hateful language/content. We have zero tolerance for bigotry. Even if you claim you're "just joking" or "didn't mean it that way," hate speech results in immediate permanent bans. Our community is welcoming to everyone, and hate has no place here.
8. No threats or encouraging real-life harm
▼
Making threats of violence, self-harm, or harm to others is taken extremely seriously - even as a joke. This includes threats against specific individuals, vague threats, or "edgy" jokes about violence. Encouraging someone to harm themselves or others is equally serious. We report credible threats to authorities. Mental health is important - if you're struggling, reach out for help, don't make threats. If you see someone making threats or expressing suicidal thoughts, report to staff immediately so we can help.
9. No sharing personal information
▼
Do not share your own or others' personal information in chat. This includes real names, addresses, phone numbers, school names, exact locations, social media accounts (unless publicly professional), or any identifying information. Sharing someone else's personal info (doxxing) results in immediate permanent bans and may be reported to authorities. Even sharing your own info is not allowed as it puts you at risk. Keep conversations about Minecraft and the server - your privacy matters.
10. No blackmail or impersonation of staff
▼
Attempting to blackmail or extort other players (threatening to expose information, threatening to get them banned unless they pay you, etc.) is completely forbidden. Impersonating staff members to gain trust, scam players, or manipulate situations is equally serious. This includes using similar names, copying staff formats, or claiming to have staff authority. Both blackmail and staff impersonation result in immediate permanent bans as they severely violate trust and safety.
11. No spreading rumors or false accusations that could hurt a staff member's reputation
▼
Making false claims about staff members (claiming they abuse power, stole items, play favorites, etc.) damages their reputation and undermines server trust. If you have a legitimate complaint about a staff member's behavior, report it privately to senior staff or the owner with evidence - don't spread it in public chat. Baseless accusations or deliberately false rumors about staff are taken very seriously. However, legitimate concerns with evidence presented properly are always investigated fairly.
12. No false reports
▼
Making deliberately false reports against other players wastes staff time and can get innocent players punished. If you're unsure whether something is rule-breaking, ask staff first before reporting. However, making a good-faith report that turns out to be mistaken is not punished - we only punish intentionally false reports. Filing multiple false reports or making reports to harass specific players results in mutes and potential bans. Report systems exist to keep the community safe, not as a weapon against players you don't like.
13. No false tickets (opening tickets without solid reasons)
▼
The Discord ticket system is for legitimate support requests, reports, appeals, and server issues - not for trolling, casual conversation, or minor complaints that belong in general chat. Opening many unnecessary tickets, opening tickets as pranks, or spamming the ticket system results in ticket access being revoked and potential mutes. Before opening a ticket, make sure you have a genuine issue that requires private staff attention. Questions like "when is next event" belong in chat, not tickets.
14. No exploiting rule loopholes
▼
Finding technical loopholes in the rules and arguing "this isn't specifically banned so it's allowed" goes against the spirit of the rules. If something seems unfair, disruptive, or wrong but isn't explicitly listed, don't do it and claim it's okay. Staff interpret rules based on intent and community wellbeing, not just literal wording. Attempting to "rules lawyer" or find loopholes results in punishment for the behavior plus additional punishment for trying to abuse the system. Rule 23 (use common sense) covers these situations.
15. Follow staff instructions
▼
When staff members ask you to stop doing something, change your behavior, or take a specific action, you must comply. This includes being told to stop certain conversations, to take discussions to DMs, to change topics, or to calm down. Arguing with staff, ignoring their instructions, or continuing banned behavior after being told to stop results in escalating punishments. If you disagree with a staff decision, comply first and then appeal through proper channels - don't argue in public chat.
16. Use common sense
▼
Chat rules can't cover every possible situation. If something seems like it would annoy people, disrupt conversations, or make the community worse, don't do it - even if it's not explicitly banned. This includes edge cases, unusual scenarios, and new forms of trolling we haven't encountered before. Ask yourself: "Is this making the chat better or worse?" If worse, don't do it. Staff use discretion to handle situations not covered by specific rules. Be respectful, considerate, and positive - that's the core principle behind all chat rules.
