Features
What makes NightFall SMP stand out from the rest.
❤️

Lifesteal

Kill players, steal their hearts. Lose all your hearts and face the ban.

When you kill a player, you steal one heart and increase your own health — up to a maximum of 20 hearts. The player you killed permanently loses one heart. If a player reaches 0 hearts, they are banned from the server. Players can also craft or trade for hearts to recover their lost health.
⚔️

PvP Main

Pure player-vs-player combat. Skill, gear, and strategy decide who lives.

PvP is the core combat mode of the server, focused entirely on player-versus-player fights. Players compete against each other using skill, gear, and strategy in a fully competitive environment. Your enemies are real players — and every fight has real consequences.
🛡️

Active Staff

A staff team that actually shows up — fair, fast, and community-focused.

Our staff team is consistently active and ready to help, handle reports, and keep the server fair for everyone. The owner is also very active and personally involved with the community — ensuring that issues are resolved quickly and the server stays a healthy place to play.
💰

Economy Based

A player-driven economy built around farming, trading, and bamboo.

The server runs on a full economy system using /shop and /ah to buy items, and /sell or direct player trading to earn currency. The server revolves around farm building — and at the heart of it all is Bamboo, the primary economic resource.
🆓

Free To Play

Zero pay-to-win. No store. No advantages for spending real money. Ever.

The server is purely free to play. There is no item, rank, or advantage that can be purchased with real-life money. We ensure the server is fair, safe, and accessible for anyone and everyone — no matter your budget. Win through skill and dedication alone.
